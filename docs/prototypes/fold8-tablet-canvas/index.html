The premise: the Fold 8's inner screen is a 7.6" 4:3 tablet — so the launcher stops rebuilding a phone on it. The wallpaper canvas is the home. The fave dock is the one fixed element; the keyboard slides in and out (⌨ button, or swipe up from the dock on device) instead of permanently owning 40% of a tablet; and everything else is freeform widgets optimized for this canvas — drag them anywhere, add more from + WIDGETS, remove in EDIT mode. Existing widgets (clock, weather, agenda) carry over; Agenda XL, Music, and People are the first tablet-sized additions.
